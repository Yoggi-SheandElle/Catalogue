Our Heritage

Where moonlight
meets gold

Born in Casablanca where ancient medina gold workshops sit blocks from modern boulevards, AYLA bridges Moroccan artistry and contemporary minimalism. Each piece is crafted with intention - meant to be worn every day.

Founded by Aya El Harch, AYLA is built on the belief that luxury should be quiet. Not loud logos, but the gentle gleam of gold that catches light when you move.
