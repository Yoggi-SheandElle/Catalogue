How It Works
From idea to live chatbot in 5 clear steps.

1. Discovery

Understand your business, users, and use cases. Identify the highest-impact conversations to automate first.

2. Design Conversation Flows

Map intents, responses, fallback paths, and human handoff rules. Define the bot's personality and knowledge scope.

3. Build & Train

Create a custom knowledge base, build the RAG pipeline, tune the model personality, and test with real conversation data.

4. Integrate

Connect to your website, Slack, WhatsApp, CRM, or helpdesk. Set up monitoring, analytics, and alert hooks.

5. Launch & Optimize

Deploy to production, monitor live conversations, and improve accuracy weekly based on real user interactions.

AI Quality & Investment
The quality of AI responses scales directly with the language model tier used. Entry-level models (GPT-3.5, Groq Llama) deliver solid results for FAQ and simple queries. Premium models (GPT-4, Claude Opus, Gemini Pro) unlock deeper reasoning, better context handling, and more natural conversations - but come at higher API costs. I always recommend the right model for your use case and budget, with transparent pricing on API costs passed through at cost.
