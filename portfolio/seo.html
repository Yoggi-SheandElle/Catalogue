My SEO Process
From audit to results in 5 clear steps.

1. Discovery

Audit current site performance, crawl structure, keyword rankings, and competitor landscape. Identify quick wins and long-term opportunities with estimated traffic impact.

2. Technical Fixes

Fix crawl errors, speed issues, broken links, duplicate content, redirect chains, and missing schema markup. Get the technical foundation right before optimizing content.

3. Content Strategy

Keyword research, content calendar planning, topic cluster mapping, and content gap analysis. Build a roadmap that targets high-intent keywords your audience is searching for.

4. On-Page Optimization

Optimize existing pages with improved meta tags, heading structure, internal links, image alt text, and keyword placement. Every page gets a custom SEO scorecard.

5. Monitoring & Iteration

Track keyword rankings, organic traffic trends, and conversion rates. Monthly reports with actionable insights and strategy adjustments based on real performance data.
