Our Story

AYLA was born in the heart of Casablanca - a city where ancient medina gold workshops sit just blocks from modern boulevards, where craftsmanship is not a trend but a way of life.

Founded by Aya El Harch, AYLA draws from years of hands-on experience in retail operations and e-commerce strategy. After managing high-volume retail at Fresh Market and leading logistics operations at MealShift, Aya understood something most designers miss: that beautiful products need operational excellence behind them to reach the people who deserve them.

That operational mind, paired with a deep love for Moroccan artistry and minimalist design, became AYLA. Every piece is designed in Casablanca with a simple philosophy: jewelry should feel like it belongs to you from the moment you put it on.

The name AYLA means "moonlight" in Arabic - a light that is soft, constant, and beautiful without trying. That's the energy behind every piece in our collection.
